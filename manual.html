Your role in one sentence You record drinks that move out of the cage into the fridge (Part A) and drinks that arrive or change inside the cage (Part B). Everything else is view-only for you.

1. Logging In

  1. Open the Fridge & Cage Drink Ops link given by your supervisor in any browser (phone or PC).
  2. You will see the Enter Access Passcode screen.
  3. Type your OPS passcode into the password box.
  4. Press Enter or click Unlock System.
  5. When you see the badge 👷 OPS MODE at the top right, you are logged in.
If the passcode is wrong A red message will appear under the button. Check with your supervisor — do not keep guessing, or you may lock the account.
Staying logged in Closing the browser tab logs you out. Opening the link again on the same device remembers your passcode for that session only.

Logging out

Click the Logout button at the top right. Always log out on shared phones or tablets.

2. Part A — Fridge Top-Up (Cage → Fridge)

Use this when you move drinks FROM the cage INTO the fridge.

  1. Click the tab 📥 PART A (FRIDGE).
  2. Check the Date field. It is filled automatically. Only change it if you are recording an earlier top-up — keep the format Mon, 25 Nov 2026, 14:30 hrs.
  3. In Updated By, choose your name (or Ops Team).
  4. On the first row, open the drink dropdown and select the drink you moved.
  5. Type the quantity in the Carton qty box. You can write 2 or 12x2 if your supervisor asks for that format.
  6. Add a Remarks note only if needed (e.g. "for event").
  7. Repeat on the next rows for other drinks.
  8. Click ✅ UPDATE FRIDGE once at the end.
What you will see after updating A green toast says Updated N record(s) for FRIDGE! and each saved row shows a small Logged badge with the quantity you entered.
Important Only click UPDATE FRIDGE once you have finished all rows. If you click it twice with the same numbers, nothing is duplicated — the system skips rows you have not changed.

Adding more rows

Click + Add Drink Row if you need more than the visible rows. Click the red ✕ to remove a row you no longer need.

Clearing the form

🧹 Clear Form wipes everything you typed but does not delete any saved records. Safe to use at the start of a new shift.

If the drink is not in the list

Choose ➕ Other / New Drink (Type Name) in the dropdown, then type the drink name in the box that appears. Tell your supervisor so ADMIN can add it to the master list.

3. Part B — Cage Stock (Incoming & Current)

Use this when a delivery arrives, or when you adjust the stock inside the cage.

  1. Click the tab 🚚 PART B (CAGE).
  2. Check the Date and Updated By fields — same as Part A.
  3. Select the drink from the dropdown.
  4. Type the quantity received or adjusted in Carton qty.
  5. Add a Remarks note for context (e.g. "delivery PO 1234", "spoilt 2 cartons").
  6. Repeat for other drinks on the same delivery.
  7. Click ✅ UPDATE CAGE.
What you will see after updating A green toast says Updated N record(s) for CAGE! and saved rows show a Logged badge.
Difference between Part A and Part B
PartMeaningExample
A — FridgeDrink leaves the cage, goes into the fridgeYou take 2 cartons of Milo from cage and put them in the fridge
B — CageDrink arrives, or cage stock changesA lorry delivers 10 cartons of Milo into the cage

4. Part C — Balance (View Only)

Check the current stock level of every drink.

  1. Click the tab 📊 PART C (BALANCE).
  2. The table shows: drink name, pack type, BALANCE (CAGE), TOPPED UP (FRIDGE), and notes.
  3. Numbers in red mean cage stock is 1 or less — tell your supervisor to reorder.
  4. Use the Sort Drink dropdown to switch between A–Z and Z–A.
  5. Click 🔄 Refresh if you think the numbers are out of date.
View only for OPS You cannot edit balances directly from Part C. If a balance looks wrong, record the correction in Part B with a clear remark, and tell ADMIN.

5. Logs (View Only)

  1. Click the tab 📋 LOGS.
  2. Every Part A and Part B record appears here, newest first.
  3. Filter with Category (All / Fridge / Cage), a date, or change the sort.
  4. Click All Dates to remove the date filter.
Why the logs matter The logs are your proof of what was moved and when. If anyone asks "who topped up the fridge on Tuesday?", the answer is here.

6. Exporting and Printing

Export to CSV (spreadsheet)

  1. Go to the LOGS tab or the PART C tab.
  2. Click 📄 Export CSV.
  3. The file downloads to your device. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets.

Print or Save as PDF

  1. Go to the tab you want (Part C or Logs).
  2. Click 🖨️ Print / Save PDF.
  3. In the browser print dialog, choose Save as PDF.
  4. Send the PDF to your supervisor on WhatsApp or email.
Tip The PDF is clean — it only shows the table and the last-updated stamp. Buttons and menus are hidden automatically.

7. Daily Checklist

Every fridge top-upRecord in Part A immediately. Do not wait until end of shift.

End of shiftOpen Part C and confirm the numbers look right. Screenshot or print if required.
Before leavingClick Logout.

8. What NOT to Do

Avoid these — they cause wrong stock numbers
  • Do not record the same top-up twice "just to be safe".
  • Do not type the quantity in the Remarks box — it must go in the Carton qty box.
  • Do not change the Date field unless you are back-filling yesterday's record and you are sure of the date.
  • Do not click UPDATE for a row where you did not change anything — it is skipped anyway, but it saves time.
  • Do not share the OPS passcode with anyone outside the team.

9. Troubleshooting

Wrong quantity already savedDo not try to fix it yourself. Tell ADMIN — they can edit the log entry directly.

3. Everyday OPS tasks (quick reminder)

As ADMIN you also record Part A and Part B the same way OPS does. See the OPS Manual sections 2 and 3 for the step-by-step. The only difference is your name in Updated By should be Admin or your real name.

4. Editing a Log Entry

Use when OPS recorded a wrong quantity, wrong drink, or wrong date.

  1. Open the 📋 LOGS tab.
  2. Use Category, date filter, or the sort dropdowns to find the row.
  3. On that row, click ✏️ Edit.
  4. A popup opens with the current values.
  5. Change only the field that is wrong:
    • Date — pick the correct date.
    • Type — FRIDGE TOP-UPS or CAGE.
    • Drink — pick the correct drink.
    • Qty — type the corrected quantity.
    • Updated By — keep or change the officer name.
  6. Click Update Log.
  7. Check the toast: ✅ Log updated! and confirm the row now shows the new values.
Editing does not create a new log The original record is replaced. If you need an audit trail of the correction, note it in a separate system (email or notebook) before you edit.

5. Deleting a Log Entry

  1. Go to the 📋 LOGS tab.
  2. Find the row you want to delete.
  3. Click the red 🗑️ button on that row.
  4. A browser confirm box appears — read it carefully, then click OK.
  5. The toast 🗑️ Log deleted. confirms it is gone.
Deletion is permanent Deleted logs cannot be recovered from the screen. The only safety net is your last .JSON backup. Always download a fresh backup before any mass clean-up.

6. Fine-Tuning a Drink Balance

Use when the stored balance is wrong and a simple delivery record will not fix it (e.g. spoilage discovered, stock count correction).

  1. Open the 📊 PART C (BALANCE) tab.
  2. Find the drink and click ✏️ Edit on its row.
  3. A popup opens with the current values.
  4. Update any of:
    • Drink Name — only change if the name is misspelled.
    • BALANCE (CAGE) — the corrected cage stock.
    • TOPPED UP (FRIDGE) — the corrected fridge stock.
    • Notes / Code — put the reason (e.g. "spoilt 3 cartons 25 Nov").
  5. Click Save Balance.
  6. Check the toast ✅ Balance saved! and confirm Part C shows the new numbers.
Write the reason in Notes A future you will look at this number and wonder why it changed. One short sentence in Notes saves an hour of guessing.

7. Adding a New Drink to the Master List

Use when a new drink type arrives that OPS could only record as "Other / New Drink".

  1. Open the ⚙️ ADMIN tab.
  2. Scroll to ➕ Add New Drink to Master List.
  3. Fill in:
    • Drink Name — use CAPITAL letters and the same style as existing drinks (e.g. MILO CAN).
    • Pack Type — already fixed as Carton; leave it.
    • BALANCE (CAGE) — starting cage stock. Use 0 if unknown.
    • Notes / Code — optional, e.g. supplier code.
  4. Click Add Drink.
  5. Check the toast ✅ Drink added! and confirm the drink now appears in the Part A / Part B dropdowns.
If the drink already exists The system may reject the duplicate. Use the ✏️ Edit button on Part C to rename or fix the existing drink instead.

8. Backup (.JSON) and Restore

Download a backup

  1. Open the ⚙️ ADMIN tab.
  2. Click ⬇️ Download .JSON Backup.
  3. A file named like dzul_art_backup_2026-11-25.json downloads.
  4. Save it somewhere safe — Google Drive, a shared folder, or email it to yourself.
When to back up Before any mass delete, before restoring anything, and at least once a week. Backups are your only recovery path.

Restore from a backup

  1. Open the ⚙️ ADMIN tab.
  2. Click ⬆️ Restore from .JSON File.
  3. Pick the backup file from your device.
  4. A confirm box appears — read it. It warns that current logs will be replaced.
  5. Click OK only if you are sure.
  6. Check the toast ✅ Backup restored! and verify Part C and Logs show the old data.
Restore replaces data Restoring overwrites the current logs with whatever is in the file. If you are not sure the file is the right one, download the current backup first so you can roll back again.

9. Export and Print

Same as OPS, with one extra option:

  • 📄 Export CSV — full movement history as a spreadsheet.
  • 🖨️ Print / Save PDF — clean printout of Part C or Logs, with last-updated stamp.
For reports to management Use Part C print — it already includes the "as of" date and last-updated time at the top. That is enough context for a daily or weekly report.

10. Safety Rules for ADMIN

Read before every destructive action
  • Back up first. Before any delete, mass edit, or restore, download a fresh .JSON backup.
  • Edit, do not delete, for small mistakes. Delete only for rows created by accident.
  • Never change the Date field to "today" just to make a row look neat. Keep the real date so audits make sense.
  • One change per action. Edit one field at a time if you are unsure, and check the toast each time.
  • Test on a slow day. If you are about to restore a backup, do it when OPS is not actively recording.
  • Keep the ADMIN passcode to yourself. Share the OPS passcode with the team instead.
